# Risks

ZeroKnow is experimental.

## Technical Risks

- Incorrect circuits can prove the wrong statement.
- Incorrect verifier integration can accept invalid proofs.
- Public inputs can leak private context.
- Metadata and timing can create linkability.
- Poor key management can compromise privacy.
- Private state systems require careful backup and recovery.

## Product Risks

- Users may misunderstand privacy as full anonymity.
- Regulated use cases may require selective disclosure.
- Developer abstractions may hide important assumptions.
- Prover marketplaces may centralize around a few operators.

## Extension Risks

The portfolio extension is not part of the paper and adds risks:

- Vault accounting risk.
- Oracle manipulation risk.
- Adapter integration risk.
- Liquidity and share pricing risk.
- Confusion between sample extension and core ZeroKnow protocol.

## Token And Utility Risk

The paper frames native utility or ecosystem functionality as potential future design space. This repo does not define token economics, equity rights, return promises, or final utility.
